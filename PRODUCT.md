# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React + Vite (user choice). Front-end only for now: demo data persisted in localStorage; backend to be connected later.

## Users
Employees of Cantine Hermes (a wine company), Italian-speaking, at desks and on phones, many not technical. They check colleagues' availability (e.g. out of office), book appointments, share calendars with groups, keep notes, and look up colleague contact details.

## Product Purpose
Internal replacement for Google Workspace Calendar. Each person decides when and with whom their own calendar is visible, so a colleague can see at a glance that someone is away. Success: anyone finds a colleague's availability or contact in seconds, with no training.

## Positioning
Visibility is owned by the calendar's owner: per-person sharing choices, group calendars, notes and company directory in one simple, company-owned tool, not a Google account.

## Operating Context
Four sections: Calendario personale, Gruppi, Note, Rubrica aziendale (all colleague contacts and data). UI language: Italian.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Personal calendar with events; owner controls who can see it.
- Groups are visibility circles, not shared calendars: members of the same group can see each other's personal calendars, and each owner chooses which of their groups may see theirs.
- Notes.
- Company directory with colleague data (role, email, phone, status/location).
- Demo data is synthetic; real people, roles and contacts are not known.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Cantine Hermes. No logo, colors or fonts provided; free to define.

## Evidence on Hand
None: no logo, no employee data, no copy. Everything shown must be labelled demo and replaceable.

## Product Principles
1. Simple before powerful: every task reachable in two clicks.
2. The owner decides visibility, and the interface always shows who can see what.
3. Availability is glanceable (in sede, fuori sede, in ferie).
4. Italian first, plain words, no jargon.
5. Equally usable on phone and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable by non-technical users of all ages; WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operable.
